Section 1
Recitals & Definitions

Background

myfiti (hereinafter "the Platform" or "Service Provider") is a gym and fitness centre management software platform offering digital tools for member management, check-in systems, subscription billing, analytics, and communication services.

ROA Fitness (hereinafter "the Client" or "Partner") is a fitness centre seeking to adopt a professional digital management solution to improve operational efficiency, member experience, and revenue tracking.

Both parties have agreed to enter into this agreement to formalise the terms under which myfiti will provide its platform services to ROA Fitness for the duration specified herein.

Definitions

  1. "Agreement" means this Partnership Agreement including all schedules and annexures.
  2. "Subscription Term" means the duration of the chosen plan as elected by the Client.
  3. "Monthly Fee" means the recurring payment due by the Client to myfiti each calendar month.
  4. "Partner Plan" means a discounted long-term plan offered exclusively to early-adopter and strategic partner clients.
  5. "Regular Plan" means the standard subscription plan available to all clients on the platform.
  6. "Platform" means the myfiti web application, mobile application, API, and all associated services hosted at app.myfiti.fit.
  7. "Data" means all information, records, and content inputted, uploaded, or generated through use of the Platform by the Client or its members.
  8. "Commencement Date" means the date on which the Client's subscription is activated on the Platform.

Payment Terms

  1. Monthly fees are due and payable on the 1st day of each calendar month.
  2. Accepted methods: Mobile Money (MTN/Orange), Bank Transfer, Cash. Reference must include the client account number.
  3. A grace period of 7 calendar days is granted before service suspension.
  4. Late payments attract a penalty of 5% per month on the outstanding balance.
  5. Payments are non-refundable once the monthly billing cycle has commenced.
  6. myfiti may suspend access after 14 days of non-payment. Reinstatement requires settlement of all outstanding balances.

Price Adjustments

The agreed monthly rate is locked for the full Subscription Term. Renewal pricing will be communicated no less than 30 days prior to the end of the term. Any renewal rate increase shall not exceed 10% above the rate applicable in the final month of the preceding term, unless agreed otherwise in writing by both parties.

Section 4
Obligations & Responsibilities

Obligations of myfiti (Service Provider)

Service Delivery
  • Maintain platform uptime of no less than 99% per month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
  • Provide at least 24 hours advance notice for any scheduled maintenance causing downtime.
  • Ensure all Client data is stored securely and backed up daily on encrypted servers.
  • Respond to support requests within 24 hours (Partner) / 48 hours (Regular) of receipt.
  • Deliver a functioning onboarding session within 5 business days of the Commencement Date.
Data & Privacy
  • Never sell, share, or disclose the Client's member or business data to any third party without explicit written consent.
  • Ensure all data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Provide data export functionality at any time during the active term.
  • Delete all Client data within 60 days of contract termination upon written request.
Third-Party Sub-Processor
  • myfiti uses Tranzak as its sole authorised payment aggregator for processing Mobile Money and digital payment transactions initiated through the platform.
  • Tranzak processes only the payment data strictly necessary to complete a transaction (amount, phone number, reference). No member personal data beyond this is shared.
  • myfiti remains fully responsible for ensuring Tranzak's handling of payment data complies with the obligations set out in this agreement.
  • The Client acknowledges and consents to the use of Tranzak as a sub-processor by entering into this agreement. No further written consent is required for this disclosed sub-processor.
  • myfiti shall notify the Client in writing at least 30 days before engaging any additional payment sub-processor not listed herein.
Platform Development
  • Continue active development and improvement of the platform throughout the agreement term.
  • Notify the Client of major feature updates or changes that may affect their workflow.
  • Not remove core features without a minimum 30-day notice and reasonable alternative.

Obligations of ROA Fitness (Client)

Payment
  • Pay the agreed monthly fee on or before the 1st of each calendar month.
  • Notify myfiti of any payment issues at least 5 business days before the due date.
  • Ensure all payments include the correct account reference number.
Platform Use
  • Use the platform only for lawful purposes in accordance with the acceptable use policy.
  • Not share admin credentials with unauthorised personnel outside registered staff accounts.
  • Not attempt to reverse-engineer, copy, or replicate any part of the myfiti platform or codebase.
  • Promptly report bugs, errors, or security concerns via official support channels.
Member Data Compliance
  • Collect and enter member data with members' informed consent.
  • Comply with applicable local data protection laws.
  • Not upload any unlawful, defamatory, or harmful content to the platform.
Partnership Conduct
  • Designate a primary contact responsible for platform management and myfiti communications.
  • Participate in at least one feedback session per quarter upon request from myfiti.
  • Not disparage myfiti, its platform, or its team in public communications during the active term.

Section 5
Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Uptime Guarantee

myfiti guarantees a platform uptime of 99% per calendar month. Downtime is measured as any period during which the platform is inaccessible to the Client due to server-side failures, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

Service Credits

  1. If uptime falls below 99% in any given month, the Client is entitled to a service credit.
  2. Credits: 97–99% uptime = 10% monthly fee credit; 95–97% = 20% credit; Below 95% = 30% credit.
  3. Credits are applied to the following month's invoice — not paid in cash.
  4. Credits must be claimed within 14 days of the end of the affected billing month.

Legal Clauses & General Provisions

Confidentiality

  1. Both parties agree to keep all proprietary, business, technical, and financial information exchanged under this agreement strictly confidential.
  2. Confidential information shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent of the disclosing party.
  3. This obligation survives termination or expiry for a period of 3 years.

Intellectual Property

  1. All intellectual property in the myfiti platform — including software code, design, logos, and documentation — remains the exclusive property of myfiti.
  2. The Client is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the platform solely for the purposes described herein.
  3. All data inputted by the Client remains the property of the Client. myfiti claims no ownership over Client or member data.

Term & Renewal

  1. This agreement commences on the Commencement Date and continues for the Subscription Term selected in Section 2.
  2. At the end of the Subscription Term, the agreement automatically transitions to month-to-month at the current standard rate unless renewed in writing.
  3. Either party may renew by providing written notice no later than 30 days before the expiry date.

Termination

  1. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice at the end of the Subscription Term.
  2. myfiti may terminate immediately and without penalty if the Client:
    1. Fails to pay outstanding fees after a 14-day grace period;
    2. Breaches the platform acceptable use policy;
    3. Engages in fraudulent, abusive, or illegal activity through the platform.
  3. The Client may terminate early with written notice; however no refund shall be issued for remaining months of the term.
  4. Upon termination, the Client shall receive a full data export within 30 days.

Limitation of Liability

  1. myfiti shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising from use or inability to use the platform.
  2. myfiti's total liability shall not exceed total monthly fees paid in the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
  3. myfiti shall not be liable for data loss caused by the Client's own actions or failure to maintain independent records.

Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held in breach for failure to perform obligations resulting from circumstances beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, internet outages, civil unrest, government directives, or natural disasters. The affected party must notify the other within 72 hours of such an event.

Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

  1. This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Cameroon, including where applicable the OHADA Uniform Acts as adopted by Cameroon.
  2. In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to first attempt resolution through good-faith negotiation within 14 calendar days of written notice of the dispute.
  3. If negotiation fails, the parties agree to pursue formal mediation facilitated by a mutually agreed mediator before commencing any legal proceedings.
  4. Should mediation fail, disputes shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Fako Division, South West Region, Cameroon. Both parties irrevocably submit to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.
  5. Notwithstanding the above, either party may seek urgent injunctive or equitable relief from any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm pending resolution of a dispute.
  6. The prevailing party in any legal proceeding shall be entitled to recover reasonable legal costs and attorney fees from the non-prevailing party.

Data Exit & Offboarding

  1. The Client retains full ownership of all data entered into the platform at all times, including member records, payment history, attendance logs, and all associated files.
  2. The Client may request a full data export at any time during the active Subscription Term. myfiti shall provide the export in CSV format within 7 business days of the request at no additional charge.
  3. Upon expiry or termination of this agreement, myfiti shall provide a complete final data export to the Client within 30 calendar days of the termination date.
  4. Following delivery of the final data export, myfiti shall retain the Client's data for a further 90 days as a recovery buffer. After this period, all Client data shall be permanently deleted from myfiti's servers and backups.
  5. myfiti shall confirm in writing when deletion is complete upon request from the Client.
  6. The Client is responsible for maintaining independent backups of critical data during the Subscription Term. myfiti's platform backups are intended for disaster recovery and do not substitute the Client's own data management obligations.

Representations & Warranties

  1. Each party represents and warrants that it has full legal authority to enter into this agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder, and that doing so does not conflict with any other agreement or obligation by which it is bound.
  2. myfiti warrants that the platform does not, to its knowledge, infringe any third-party intellectual property rights, and that it will maintain the platform in a functional and professionally operable state throughout the Subscription Term.
  3. The Client warrants that all member data entered into the platform has been collected with the informed consent of the individuals concerned, and that the Client is authorised to process and share such data with myfiti for the purposes described herein.
  4. Neither party makes any representations or warranties beyond those expressly stated in this agreement.

Non-Solicitation

During the Subscription Term and for a period of 12 months following its expiry or termination, neither party shall directly solicit, recruit, or employ any employee, contractor, or consultant of the other party who was involved in the performance of this agreement, without the prior written consent of that party.
  2. This clause does not restrict either party from hiring individuals who respond to general public job advertisements not specifically targeting the other party's staff.
