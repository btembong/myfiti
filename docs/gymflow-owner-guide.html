01 — Getting Started

Onboarding & Setup

myfiti uses a multi-tenant architecture — each gym gets its own isolated workspace with its own members, data, branding, and settings. Onboarding takes under five minutes and gives you a fully operational gym management system.

Registration Flow

1

Create your account

Visit app.myfiti.app/register. Enter your name, business email, gym name, and a password. A confirmation email is sent immediately.

2

Gym profile

After verifying your email, you are taken to the onboarding wizard. Enter your gym's name, country, timezone, and currency (default: XAF for Cameroon).

3

Branding

Upload a logo and choose your primary brand color. This color appears throughout your member-facing kiosk and mobile app.

4

Create your first plan

Define at least one membership plan (name, price, duration). You can add more plans at any time from Settings → Plans.

5

Your workspace is ready

You land on your dashboard. A unique slug (e.g. korafit) is assigned — this is used to scope all your data and for the member app URL.

✓
14-day free trial. All features are available from day one. No credit card required to start.

What Gets Created

When your gym is registered, the platform automatically provisions:

ResourceDescription
Tenant schemaA dedicated database namespace (tenant_korafit) — your data is completely isolated from other gyms.
Gym settingsName, slug, currency, timezone, logo, brand color, grace period settings.
Staff accountYour account is created as Owner — the highest permission level.
Kiosk URLA signed kiosk URL is generated for your self-service terminal.

Referral System

Each member gets a unique referral code visible in their mobile app. When a new member signs up using that code, the referrer's wallet is automatically credited with a reward (default: 500 XAF) once the referred member makes their first payment. Referral stats — total referrals, conversion rate, pending vs converted, total rewards issued — are available in Analytics → Referrals.

Member Wallet

Every member has an internal wallet balance. The wallet is credited automatically when a referral reward is earned, or when the gym manually adds credit. Members can view their balance and transaction history in the mobile app (Wallet screen). Staff can view the wallet balance from the member's profile in the admin panel (Payments → Wallet tab).

05 — Subscriptions

Subscriptions & Lifecycle

Every member's relationship with a plan is tracked as a subscription. The system automatically manages status transitions, sends reminder emails, and handles grace periods — all without any manual work.

The subscription cron job runs every hour and handles all transitions automatically. You never need to manually expire or suspend memberships.

Renewal

When a payment is recorded (any method), the subscription is automatically extended:

  • If active or expiring: the new expiry is calculated from the current expiry date (no lost days).
  • If expired or suspended: the new expiry is calculated from today.

Grace Period

The grace period gives members extra time to renew without losing access. Default is 7 days. During grace, the kiosk shows an amber warning screen — the member can still enter, but is reminded to renew. Configurable in Settings → Gym settings.

Freeze (Holiday Hold)

If a member is traveling or injured, staff can freeze their subscription from the member profile. The system sets frozen_until and pauses the expiry timer. Kiosk access is blocked during the freeze.

Auto-renew Flag

Each subscription has an auto_renew flag, visible to the member in the mobile app. In future, this will be used to trigger automatic Tranzak charges on expiry.

Used in the kiosk day pass flow and the member mobile app renewal. The system sends a USSD prompt directly to the customer's phone. No link, no QR scan, no browser. The customer simply enters their PIN.

08 — Payments

Member Wallet

Every member has a built-in digital wallet. Members can top up their balance via Mobile Money, send funds to other members, and pay for subscriptions or day passes directly from their wallet balance.

What Members Can Do

↑

Top Up

Add funds via MTN MoMo or Orange Money USSD push. A USSD prompt is sent to the member's phone — they enter their PIN and the balance updates instantly.

→

Send to Member

Transfer funds to another gym member by searching their name or phone number. The recipient's balance updates immediately.

↓

Cash Out

Withdraw the wallet balance to a Mobile Money number via USSD push. Funds arrive within seconds.

✓

Pay for Services

Members can use their wallet balance to pay for subscriptions and day passes at checkout, without re-entering payment details.

Transaction History

Every wallet operation (top-up, send, receive, cashout, payment) is logged with a timestamp, amount, and counterparty. Members can view their full history in the Wallet tab of the mobile app.

Wallet Reconciliation

A nightly automated job runs at 01:00 to cross-check wallet balances against transaction history and flag any discrepancies.

✓
Atomic balances. All wallet operations use database-level atomic transactions — a top-up and a concurrent send can never conflict. The balance is always consistent.

18 — Automation

Automated Jobs

Two background processes run continuously on the server, handling all time-sensitive operations automatically. You do not need to trigger anything manually.

Subscription Cron — Every Hour

Runs every 60 minutes across all active gyms and transitions subscription statuses:

JobConditionAction
Expiry warning Active subscription expires in ≤ 7 days Status → expiring_soon. Renewal reminder email queued.
Grace period Subscription past expiry, within grace window Status → grace_period. Grace alert email queued. Member status updated.
Suspension Grace period has also expired Status → suspended. Suspension notice email queued. Member status updated. Kiosk access blocked.
ℹ
If the cron shows "0 transitions" in the server log, that simply means no subscriptions crossed a threshold in that hour. This is normal.

Billing Cron — Every 6 Hours

Motivation Cron — Daily at 07:00

Sends a personalised motivational push notification to every member with an active subscription once per day. Messages vary by day of week and are tailored to encourage gym attendance.

✓

Wallet Reconciliation Cron — Daily at 01:00

Runs every night across all tenants and cross-checks each member's stored wallet balance against the sum of their transaction ledger. Any mismatch is flagged in the server log for review. Normal operation logs "0 discrepancies".

Queue System (BullMQ)

Email and push notifications are queued through BullMQ with Redis (Upstash). If the queue is temporarily unavailable, status transitions in the database still happen — only the notification is delayed. It will be retried automatically.

Every new gym gets a 14-day free trial with full access. 7 days before the trial ends, the owner receives a warning email. When the trial expires, the account transitions to past_due — a grace period applies before access is restricted.
